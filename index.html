Running Engine Thesis

Accumulation wins
8 km
Race running volume

HYROX is not a lifting event with some jogging. It is eight separate 1 km runs with stations interrupting rhythm, breathing, and leg turnover.

100+
Monthly mile floor

The goal is not one heroic run. The goal is repeatable accumulation: steady miles, controlled fatigue, and enough durability to keep running late in the race.

13K-20K
Monthly vertical target

Elevation is the edge-builder: leg durability, cardiac output, mental toughness, and fatigue resistance. It supports HYROX, but it does not replace flat compromised 1 km practice.

What we learned: HYROX looks like a lifting race, but the runners usually decide the ceiling. Strength keeps the stations from becoming disasters; running capacity keeps the whole race from bleeding minutes.

Principles

Non-negotiables

Not bench. Not deadlift. Not squat. Not even 5K. 1 km after a sled push.

Run 1 km. Push the sled. Run another 1 km. The difference tells us whether strength is helping or fatigue is stealing the race.

Running first. Strength maintenance second.

Both athletes are strong enough. The team gets fastest when the running floor rises and station execution becomes automatic.
